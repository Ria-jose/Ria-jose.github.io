🌙 My Midnight Workshop Journey

When the harvest moon rises above the crooked rooftops and the first owl hoots, I wake up with a sleepy yawn and polish my glowing amber eyes. Every night, I wind up my phantom music box before tiptoeing to the kitchen for a mug of warm marshmallow hot chocolate. Then it's time for mama to give me a bubble bath. Next, I float down to my candlelit workshop to fill my glass jars with pumpkin-spice dreams. Once every jar is glowing, I teleport from chimney to chimney delivering sweet dreams to all my little ghost friends—before curling up at dawn for my morning nap!
