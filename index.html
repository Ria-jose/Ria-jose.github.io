My Journey

When the sun dips below the rooftops and the first cricket chirps, I wake up with a yawn and polish my amber eyes. Every evening I turn on my music box before going to the kitchen to drink a hot chocolate. Then it's time for mama to give me a bath. Next, I go down to my workshop and fill my dream jars. After filling all the jars for the night, I teleport to chimneys and deliver the dreams. At dawn, I go back home for my morning nap.
